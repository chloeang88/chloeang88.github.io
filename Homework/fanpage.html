a little corner just for her ✿

There's something so dreamy about the way Elle Fanning moves through a role — one moment she's whimsical and glowing, the next she's quietly devastating, and somehow it always feels effortless. From Maleficent's Princess Aurora to The Great's unhinged, brilliant Catherine, she brings this soft, curious, slightly chaotic sparkle to everything she touches. She's just so cute and so talented, and I genuinely adore her acting — this whole page is a little love letter to that.

fun facts ✿

01

Ryan Gosling forever

Elle has had a soft spot for Ryan Gosling since she was a teenager, once saying his movie The Ides of March is a favorite and that she and her sister Dakota had matching Ryan Gosling coloring books. Iconic behavior, honestly.

02

her Jack Black era

Elle has openly declared Jack Black her celebrity "hall pass," calling him the "hottest man" she's ever seen. When they finally met at the Golden Globes, she said it "made her life." Bestie behavior.

03

go-to order

Her favorite food is a classic Cobb salad — though she's also confessed to a serious strawberry obsession and a love of extra mayo on the side, always.

04

the acting range

From child star in Super 8 and Somewhere to Catherine the Great in The Great and Rachel in Sentimental Value — she just keeps getting more whimsical and more brilliant with every role.
